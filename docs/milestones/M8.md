# M8 — Live-Readiness Review (Go/No-Go)

> Draft work order (Claude, 2026-08-19) — replace with the §4-template version when provided.

**Status:** ⏳ planned · **Depends on:** M1–M7 (all of it — this is the graduation gate)

## Goal

A written, evidence-backed go/no-go decision on trading a small amount of real
money — made by Marty, never by the bot or an AI agent, per constitution rules 2
and 3 and the hardcoded `paper=True`.

## Why now (last)

Everything before this exists to make this decision boring: tested rails (M1),
trustworthy data (M2–M3), a measured model (M4), a chosen strategy (M5), a
process that stays up (M6), and visibility (M7).

## Scope

**In:**
- A go/no-go checklist completed in writing, proposal:
  - [ ] M1 suite green on the exact commit to be deployed.
  - [ ] ≥ N weeks (proposal: 4) of supervised paper trading with zero
        constitution violations and zero unexplained halts.
  - [ ] Slippage report from `logs/slippage.csv`: paper fills vs quotes, with a
        written estimate of expected live degradation.
  - [ ] Drawdown stayed inside `review_drawdown_pct` for the whole window.
  - [ ] Kill-switch fire drill performed (trip it live, verify halt, hand-reset).
  - [ ] Live dollar allocation and per-trade risk chosen (**TBD by Marty** — the
        $9,000 paper balance is not the proposal).
- The switch itself: a deliberate, single commit changing `paper=True`, made only
  after the signed-off checklist, with limits retuned to the live balance first.
- A rollback rule: any constitution violation live ⇒ kill switch + back to paper.

**Out:** automating any part of the decision. No agent, scheduler, or config flag
may flip paper → live. This document is the process *because* the code refuses to be.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The completed checklist lives in this file (or a dated copy) with Marty's
      explicit written go/no-go and date.
- [ ] If "go": live trading starts at the chosen small size with all limits
      re-derived from the real balance.
- [ ] If "no-go": the blocking items are listed and fed back into the plan.

## Risks & guardrails

- Paper fills are optimistic — assume live is worse than the slippage report says.
- Smallest viable size first; scaling up is its own future decision, not a default.
