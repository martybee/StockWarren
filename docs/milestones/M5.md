# M5 — Strategy Comparison & Winner Decision

> Draft work order (Claude, 2026-08-19) — replace with the §4-template version when provided.

**Status:** ⏳ planned · **Depends on:** M3 (trade store), M4 (models trained or explicitly excluded from the comparison)

## Goal

Decide, with a rule written **before** the results exist, which of
alpha/beta/gamma's strategy parameters wins the A/B/C experiment — then promote
the winner to the canonical config.

## Why now

The three accounts exist precisely for this comparison, and the only way the
comparison is honest is pre-registration: metric, sample size, and tie-breaks
chosen now, while all three still look equal.

## Scope

**In:**
- A written decision rule in this file, agreed by Marty, e.g.:
  minimum **N completed trades per account** (proposal: 100) or a fixed window,
  primary metric (proposal: profit factor with a max-drawdown cap), tie-break
  order, and disqualification triggers (shutdown-drawdown trip = out).
- Compare page shows the chosen metric(s) per account, plus trade count toward N,
  fed from the M3 store.
- The decision itself recorded in `PLAN.md` + `SESSION_NOTES.md` with the numbers
  that drove it; losing configs archived in git, not deleted.

**Out:** mid-experiment parameter tweaks to any account (that invalidates the
comparison — changes wait for the next experiment round).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Decision rule filled in below and confirmed by Marty **before** any account
      hits N/2 trades.
- [ ] Leaderboard shows the pre-registered metric, not just raw P&L.
- [ ] A written winner decision exists (or a written "no winner, iterate" outcome).

## The decision rule (pre-registered 2026-08-19 — frozen)

Pasted from CHART_PLAN §14 Decision 4:

- Minimum trades per account: **N = 100 completed trades**, with **no interim
  judging before 50**.
- Primary metric: **profit factor, subject to a max-drawdown cap.** The cap is a
  **frozen literal**, immune to later config overrides: **TBD-DISCREPANCY-HOUR**
  (during the discrepancy hour, read each account's configured
  `review_drawdown_pct` and write the smallest here as a number).
- Disqualifiers: hitting `shutdown_drawdown_pct` = **disqualified**. A
  constitution violation or unexplained halt = **pause the whole experiment**
  (kill switch, fix, written decision to resume or restart) rather than DQ'ing
  the unlucky account — violations are shared-code bugs.
- Tie-break: **lower max drawdown wins.**

## Risks & guardrails

- Watch for regime luck: three accounts trading the same 10 symbols in the same
  weeks share one market regime. A winner here is "best in this regime," not best
  forever — say so in the decision write-up.
