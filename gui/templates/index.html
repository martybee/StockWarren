    📊 Signals

    Seven technical indicators each vote buy / sell / neutral on every watched symbol. A weighted composite must clear your strength and confirmation thresholds before a trade is proposed.

    🤖 ML Filter

    A Random-Forest model learns from completed trades and can veto weak signals. It starts untrained (approving all signals) and retrains as samples accumulate.

    🌅 EOD Manager

    Before the market close, the End-of-Day manager flattens day-trade positions so nothing is held overnight unintentionally. Swing positions are spared; untracked positions are closed defensively.

    🔔 Notifications

    Optional Discord and email alerts on trade entries, exits, and errors. Credentials are configured in the .env file; toggles live in config/settings.ini.
